Remediation Recommendations

Actionable, prioritized engineering steps to eliminate detected protocol and configuration weaknesses.

Maintain AES-GCM Authenticated Encryption

Your active IKE and ESP tunnels are operating using AES-256-GCM with 16-byte ICV. Ensure all fallback proposals to 3DES or DES remain completely disabled on the VPN gateway.

Future Post-Quantum Readiness

Diffie-Hellman Group 14 (2048-bit MODP) provides adequate classical security. Consider preparing an upgrade plan for DH Group 19 (256-bit ECP) or hybrid post-quantum key exchange (RFC 9370).
